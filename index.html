The Golden Rule

Don't restart the entire plan or try to make up missed workouts. Resume from where you left off.

Time Away What to do when you return
1–2 days Continue with your next scheduled workout.
3–5 days Resume the next workout in your split. If you feel recovered, use your normal weights with good form; reduce the load if needed.
6–10 days Consider starting with around 80–90% of your usual load and slightly less volume.
More than 10–14 days Return gradually, using lighter weights and fewer sets until your strength and stamina recover.
1–2 Days Missed Zero Impact

Treat them as unplanned rest days. Continue with your next scheduled workout at 100%.

3–5 Days Missed Slight Buffer

Resume the next workout in your split. If you feel recovered, use your normal weights with good form; reduce the load if needed.

6–10 Days Missed Ramp-Up

Consider starting with around 80–90% of your usual load and slightly less volume.

> 10–14 Days Missed Gradual Reset

Return gradually, using lighter weights and fewer sets until your strength and stamina recover.

Practical Field Example:

Suppose you completed Day 2 (Pull), then missed the gym for four days:

  • Your next workout is Day 3 — Legs.
  • After that, continue with Day 4 — Rest.
  • Proceed to Day 5 — Upper.
  • Continue the schedule normally without doing double workouts.

You do not need to repeat Day 1 just because you took a break. If you are sick, injured, or still feeling unusually fatigued, recover first and return gradually.

01 / Warm-up

5–10 minutes of light cardio, followed by warm-up sets before heavy work.

02 / Rest Between Sets

60–120 seconds; allow longer for demanding compound exercises.

03 / Weight Selection

Choose a weight that lets you complete each rep with controlled, proper form.

04 / Progressive Overload

Gradually increase reps or weight as exercises become easier.

05 / Consistency

Missing a few days will not ruin your progress. Avoid doubling your workload to compensate.

06 / Training Frequency

Follow the split in order, but don't force yourself to train seven consecutive calendar days.

Important: This is a suggested exercise plan. Adjust the exercise selection and training volume to your experience and recovery.
