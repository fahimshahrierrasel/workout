The Cardinal Rule

Do not restart the entire program. Never attempt to combine multiple missed sessions into one day. Resume exactly where you left off.

1 - 2 Days Missed ZERO IMPACT

Treat them as unplanned rest days. Pick up the next scheduled workout at 100% load. No volume adjustments needed.

3 - 5 Days Missed SLIGHT INTENSITY BUFFER

Resume on the next workout. Reduce total working sets by 1 set per exercise on Day 1 to avoid crippling delayed soreness (DOMS).

6 - 10 Days Missed RAMP-UP CADENCE

Resume split order. Cut load by 10-15% for the first week to recondition connective tendons and muscular motor pathways.

> 10 - 14 Days Missed FULL RESET INTRO

Run a 1-week reload phase at 70% intensity across all days before progressing back to top personal-record targets.

Field Example

If you finish Monday (Push), then fall ill for 4 days through Friday: on Saturday, you do NOT jump to Saturday's shoulder routine. You perform Tuesday (Pull).

01. Dynamic Warm-Up 5-8 MIN

Raise core body temperature before touching a working barbell. Perform 2 ramp sets at 40% and 70% load prior to your first counted working set.

02. Rest Intervals 90S - 3M

Compounds (Squats, Presses, Rows) demand 2 to 3 full minutes of ATP replenishment. Isolation movements (Curls, Raises) thrive on 60 to 90 seconds.

03. Weight Selection RPE 7.5 - 9

Select a weight that leaves 1-2 repetitions in reserve (RIR). Never train to complete technical failure on free-weight spinal loading movements.

04. Progressive Overload +1 REP OR +2.5%

Add one repetition within your target bracket before bumping plate weight. When you hit the top rep ceiling on all sets, increase load next session.

05. Consistency Over Guilt LONG GAME

A mediocre session performed today beats an optimal session delayed to next week. Accumulate reps calmly; progress is mechanical arithmetic.

06. Cadence Control 3-0-1 TEMPO

Control the negative portion for 2-3 seconds. Eliminate bounce or recoil momentum. Muscle hypertrophy triggers heavily through loaded stretch.
