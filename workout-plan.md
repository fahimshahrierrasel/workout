### What to do if you miss the gym for 3–5 days

The rule: Don't restart the entire plan or try to make up missed workouts. Resume from where you left off.

| Time away            | What to do when you return                                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–2 days             | Continue with your next scheduled workout.                                                                                       |
| 3–5 days             | Resume the next workout in your split. If you feel recovered, use your normal weights with good form; reduce the load if needed. |
| 6–10 days            | Consider starting with around 80–90% of your usual load and slightly less volume.                                                |
| More than 10–14 days | Return gradually, using lighter weights and fewer sets until your strength and stamina recover.                                  |

### Example

Suppose you completed Day 2 (Pull), then missed the gym for four days.

* Your next workout is Day 3 — Legs & Abs.

* After that, continue with Day 4 — Rest.

* Proceed to Day 5 — Chest, Back & Arms.

* Continue the schedule normally.

You do not need to repeat Day 1 just because you took a break.

If you are sick, injured, or still feeling unusually fatigued, recover first and return gradually.

### General training guidelines

* Warm-up: 5–10 minutes of light cardio, followed by warm-up sets.

* Rest between sets: 60–120 seconds; allow longer for demanding compound exercises.

* Weight selection: Choose a weight that lets you complete each rep with controlled, proper form.

* Progressive overload: Gradually increase reps or weight as exercises become easier.

* Consistency: Missing a few days will not ruin your progress. Avoid doubling your workload to compensate.

* Training frequency: Follow the split in order, but don't force yourself to train seven consecutive calendar days.
